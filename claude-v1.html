05 Where to start
If you do one thing

Clone laguagu / claude-code-nextjs-skills and read every SKILL.md — it's the cleanest overlap with what modern teams use (App Router, RSC, Vercel AI SDK, streaming, SEO).

Then adopt the Front Kit accessibility workflow + Claudia Ng's CLAUDE.md guardrails. Build one or two distinctive, WCAG-AA Next.js projects and document your workflow — a reproducible, AI-assisted process is far more valuable than a one-off demo.

Skip the generic "built a todo app" content entirely — it teaches almost nothing about shipping real software.
